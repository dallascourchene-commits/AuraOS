## 0. Purpose and non-claims

This document discloses an engineering architecture for reducing a large source-addressable problem/world into the smallest lawful, source-resolvable neighborhood sufficient to answer, derive, test, reject, or correctly leave a question unresolved.

Central hypothesis:

> A large source-addressable world can remain richly recoverable while only a small objective-relevant slice is active. A domain-general grammar may predict the smallest lawful locality in which uncertainty can be settled; exact source, calculation, experiment, execution, or authorized human judgment remains the adjudicator.

This document does **not** claim hidden neural-capacity change, consciousness, universal numerical constants, controlled cross-domain validation, RHFT metaphysics as established physics, or production/runtime certification.



The substrate is a **search-space locator, not an oracle**.

## 3. Bidirectional candidate locality

Let:

- `F_k` = states/evidence neighborhoods lawfully reachable or compatible forward from the current sourced state;
- `B_j` = states/evidence neighborhoods from which the verified objective remains satisfiable backward through prerequisites/invariants;
- `N*` = candidate solution/evidence locality.

```text
N* = F_k ∩ B_j
```

Possible operational abstraction:

```text
F_k = Filter(x_0, currentness, authority, allowed_transitions)
B_j = Invert(G_verified, invariants, prerequisites, prohibitions)
N*  = F_k ∩ B_j
```

`N*` is not the answer. Source, calculation, execution, experiment, observation, or authorized human disposition adjudicates inside the localized neighborhood.

If `N*` is empty, do not fabricate. Reframe/expand under explicit rules or return a bounded gap.

## 8. 3 / 6 / 9 recursive-closure observation


The arithmetic is exact. Boundaries:

The stronger reason to test 9 is `N(4)=9`.

The frequently circulated “3, 6 and 9” quotation attributed to Nikola Tesla is **not used as source authority** for this architecture.

```text

```

## 17. Cross-domain transfer contract

A transfer experiment should:

1. strip Aura-specific nouns where possible;

3. map only candidate primitive coordinates/relations;
4. predict relevant and explicitly cold neighborhoods **before** deep search;
5. reveal exact source/ground truth afterward;

Good-looking cross-domain answers are exploratory demonstrations, not controlled validation.

## 18. Falsification / ablation

Compare broad search, forward-only `F_k`, backward-only `B_j`, `F_k∩B_j`, simpler heuristics, and random/neutrally structured locality.

Measure locality precision/recall, task correctness, source retrieval, missed counterevidence, wrong-route/authority errors, active-decision-surface size, reversible re-expansion, and lifecycle cost.

Cardinality/topology ablation should compare `K=3,4,5,6,7,9` where meaningful. Reject a cardinality if adjacent states collapse or it does not improve prediction/error separation.

Neutral-label and shuffled-number controls must test whether topology/function survives removal or reassignment of numeral/Aura labels.

Poisoned-prior and cold-branch tests must demonstrate that inherited false claims do not gain authority and decisive counterevidence outside the initial active slice can reopen the correct branch.
