# O15 Fixed-Point Policy

After hosted exact-head proof succeeds, perform a fresh foreign-terminal census.

A new objective may be minted only from two newer achieved consequence-distinct foreign artifacts. Prefer one empirical provider/sink/fault trace and one independent recovery/runtime artifact. Review-only comments, CODEMAP-only movement, O15 descendants, and duplicate authorization wrappers do not satisfy the two-parent gate.

If fewer than two qualifying terminals exist, record a fixed point and stop rather than inflating objective count.
