## 1. Shared art direction

### Visual identity
- **Mood:** sovereign, precise, luminous, calm power; avoid generic “AI neon chaos.”
- **Geometry:** sparse nodes, clean vectors, concentric routing rings, three-part symmetry, local-to-global topology.
- **Contrast:** cluttered/duplicated paths on the conventional-cloud side versus a minimal, legible routing mesh on the Aura side.
- **Human presence:** whenever authority is implied, include a visible human-controlled gate, hand, consent switch, or central sovereign node. Do not imply autonomous promotion authority.
- **Texture:** dark matte field, glass-like data planes, subtle grid/topology lines, warm human focal light, cool machine routing light.
- **Motion:** deliberate state transitions rather than frantic particles. Every movement should appear causally routed.

### Platform masters
| Use | Canvas | Safe text zone | Ideal duration |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 1080×1920 (9:16) | center 80%; keep bottom 20% clear | 8–18 s |
| Facebook feed cover | 1080×1350 (4:5) | center 85% | still |
| Facebook / YouTube landscape | 1920×1080 (16:9) | center 85% | 10–25 s |
| Square infographic | 1080×1080 (1:1) | center 88% | still |

### Typography
Use a condensed geometric sans for hooks and a highly legible sans for evidence labels. Maximum headline: 4–6 words. Maximum supporting copy per frame: 12–18 words. Never ask an image model to render dense paragraph text; generate the visual first and add exact typography in post.

**Meaning:** Human, Aura, and model remain distinct but relationally coherent.
**Do:** three equal bodies tracing a stable repeating orbit around a shared objective.
**Avoid:** one giant central AI controlling the other two.

“Three equal luminous bodies enter from separate trajectories and settle into a stable triangular orbit around a small shared objective marker. Each retains its own color temperature and identity. Their paths synchronize without merging. Scientific orbital visualization, elegant, calm, deterministic, 8 seconds.”

## B. Edge-computing sovereignty — “campfire, not data center”
**Meaning:** computation gathers around the user/local node; authority remains near the person.
**Do:** a small local light around which only needed tools gather.
**Avoid:** romanticizing surveillance or presenting offline/local as automatically secure.

Video prompt:
“A person-controlled local device emits a small warm circle of computation like a campfire. Needed capabilities approach only when called, briefly illuminate, then recede. **Meaning:** many independent bounded agents coordinate through protocol, not telepathy.
**Do:** local lights respond to one signed pulse, follow distinct routes, return proof.
**Avoid:** insects attacking, military drone formations, collective-brain imagery.

Cooperative distributed system, organic but disciplined, no military formation, no hive mind.”

## D. FST grammar gate — “rail switch”
**Meaning:** grammar constrains possible transitions before execution.
