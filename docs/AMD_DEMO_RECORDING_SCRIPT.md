# Full narration script

AuraOS is a sovereign, local-first cognitive operating system for human and AI collaboration. Models are replaceable workers. Evidence, verification, and people retain authority.

Most AI agents receive excessive context and unclear authority. Aura creates a bounded Arena for each objective, gives a worker only the required capabilities, verifies the result, and keeps consequential decisions under human or community governance.

This is the Winnipeg Community Pathways Lab, using synthetic data only. Aura can map aggregate service access, public infrastructure, capacity, and scenario locations, but it refuses person-level maps of homelessness, addiction, health, poverty, crime, or Indigenous identity.

MITOSIS decomposes the objective while preserving mandatory constraints. MUSIC compares four approaches and exposes trade-offs rather than secretly choosing a winner. Objections and representation gaps remain visible. The result is a reversible pilot proposal, not an AI decision for the community.

The map behavior creates a real product question. Aura does not blindly call it a bug. It distinguishes policy, fixture data, and presentation intent.

The Human Agent Arena grounds the issue in exact files, hashes, source ranges, and tests. It proposes alternatives and stages a review-only solution. Nothing is automatically committed, pushed, or merged.

In the Sovereign Learning Arena, the objective becomes a structured six-slot packet and passes through guarded routing. The exact file and verifier boundaries are declared before a worker acts.

The first task passes independent verification and becomes a verified crystal. The related second task retrieves that procedure as advice and passes its own verifier. The original source checkout remains unchanged and the temporary workspace dissolves.

For AMD ACT II, Aura implements a Gemma worker path through AMD ROCm or AMD-hosted Fireworks inference, followed by bounded execution, detached verification, verified crystals, and optional PEFT or LoRA training from proven results.

Aura is not one application. It is a substrate for many sovereign Arenas.

In disaster relief, a community, shelter, health team, transportation provider, humanitarian organization, and emergency government office could each retain its own data and authority while sharing minimum verified resource and need capsules. Frontline workers could see shelter capacity, roads, supplies, transport, assignments, and unresolved needs even through intermittent connectivity. The system remains useful offline without an LLM and synchronizes signed updates when links return.

The same pattern can coordinate government or regulated institutions. Health, infrastructure, budgets, social policy, emergency management, and Indigenous or treaty-obligation Arenas remain isolated until one lawful objective requires a temporary federation.

A future social information Arena can cluster licensed public and consented discussions by intent rather than engagement. VSA points to topic constellations, while exact posts, sources, permissions, timestamps, and deletion state remain in sidecars.

Existing tools map crises, integrate government data, or detect social events. Aura's opportunity is to combine those capabilities under one governed, local-first composition layer: vectors point, sidecars prove, and people authorize.
