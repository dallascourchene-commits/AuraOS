# Aura Cognitive Substrate Contract — P9

This document summarizes the public contract assembled through P1–P8 of issue #93. It is documentation and verification evidence. It does not activate a planner, migrate a caller, transfer a store, authorize an action, publish a package, or change a live owner.

## Core rule

Planning proposes. Governance authorizes. Verification proves. Exact source spans and hashes remain patch authority. Planning candidates, continuity packets, compact representations, compatibility evidence, and domain shadows remain advisory.

## Layers

1. **P1–P1.1:** deterministic event, tool-decision, exact-sidecar, privacy, and action-bound authority contracts.
2. **P2.1–P2.3:** immutable Planning Board IR, bounded backward regression, and forward symbolic replay over public state.
3. **P3.1–P3.2:** append-only planning events and a read-only history integrity projector.
4. **P4.1–P5.3:** compact J2 continuity plus canonical ST3GG V2 contracts and V1-retaining compatibility facades.
5. **P6.1–P6.2:** proposal-only QDKT observations and opt-in dual-read verification of an unchanged legacy result.
6. **P7–P8:** read-only Coding Arena and Civic Commons Planning Board shadows with retained live owners.

## Continuity

Planning Board continuity is namespaced `BC0_STRUCTURAL` through `BC5_VERIFIED`. The levels cover structure, typing, constraints, grounding, external authorization references, and verifier receipts. Even BC5 does not execute an action.

## Ownership

Contract adoption and live ownership are separate facts. P9 records contract status, compatibility mode, migration status, live owner, and ownership disposition independently. Parity, exact recall, or a verified shadow does not imply migration.
