# Aura Civic Governance and Consent

## Consent Arc

The Consent Arc is NON_BINDING, BOUNDED_TO_RECORDED_PARTICIPANTS, NOT_A_REFERENDUM, NOT_REPRESENTATIVE_UNLESS_PROVEN.

It displays support, consent, consent with reservations, needs more information, objection, critical objection, abstention, participation scope, and missing/underrepresented groups.

It is never reduced to a single percentage. An accessible table equivalent is always provided.

## Consensus Statuses

DRAFT, COLLECTING_INPUT, EMERGING_ALIGNMENT, BROAD_CONSENT, CONSENT_WITH_RESERVATIONS, CONTESTED, BLOCKED_BY_CRITICAL_OBJECTION, INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION, INSUFFICIENT_PARTICIPATION, REPRESENTATION_GAP, LEGAL_OR_RESOURCE_BLOCK, PILOT_RECOMMENDED, NO_DECISION, CLOSED_BY_HUMAN_FACILITATOR

## Dissent Preservation

Every final packet includes reservations, unresolved objections, critical objections, minority report, affected-group concerns, alternatives not selected, why options changed rank, participant scope, missing voices, and what evidence could change the outcome.

Never output "The community agrees." without displaying the exact participant scope and evidence.

## Democratic Friction

Tracks round count, meeting count, elapsed time, proposal revisions, evidence requests, objections raised/addressed/unresolved, facilitator interventions, outreach actions, participant turnover, and unresolved representation gaps.

Friction is not scored as democratic failure.

## Authority Boundaries

Aura may: retrieve, cite, describe, compare, summarize, map, match, simulate, surface trade-offs, propose scenarios, propose bridge options, prepare decision packets.

Aura may not: enact, approve, license, legislate, spend public money, transfer property, bind a participant, submit an official vote, manufacture consensus, erase dissent, claim legal compliance, claim representativeness without evidence, represent simulation as observation.
