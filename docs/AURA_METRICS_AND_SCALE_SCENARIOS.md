# Aura Metrics and Scale Scenarios — Deprecated Historical Appendix

**System:** Aura — Augmented Universal Reasoning Architecture  
**Deprecated as active evidence:** 2026-08-08  
**Status:** historical pointer only

---

## Why this document was deprecated

Earlier versions of this document combined several useful but very different things:

- measured Aura benchmark results;
- deterministic token/context proxies;
- synthetic continuity experiments;
- counterfactual savings calculations;
- hypothetical developer-adoption arithmetic;
- global data-centre energy scenarios;
- external edge-compute research.

Even when individually labeled, placing all of those numbers in one active metrics document made it too easy for a human or AI reader to collapse **benchmark evidence** and **scenario arithmetic** into one apparent Aura performance claim.

That is no longer the evidence policy.

## What is no longer an active Aura claim

Do **not** cite the former contents of this file as current evidence for:

- giant generalized efficiency multipliers;
- global TWh/year savings;
- hypothetical adoption or developer-scale productivity;
- large counterfactual Council-amortization percentages;
- provider billing or provider token savings inferred from token proxies;
- Aura energy savings inferred from external hardware papers;
- architecture-wide performance inferred from a synthetic subsystem fixture.

Those were scenario analyses, historical experiments, or external context — not measurements of Aura's integrated current architecture.

If any of those questions become important again, answer them with a dedicated benchmark rather than restoring a projection as a headline.

## External research remains useful — in a separate lane

Research such as RouteLLM, repository-level context-compression studies, AgentRadio, SkillComposer, Proof-Carrying Agent Actions, Verifiable Memory, When Self-Evolution Backfires, When History Lies, TRAJDEBUG, HarnessOpt-Bench, and QEIL provides benchmark evidence for **similar architectural pressures**.

Their results remain the results of those projects.

They are cataloged as **external comparable benchmarks** in [`AURA_EVIDENCE_MAP.md`](AURA_EVIDENCE_MAP.md), where each external number stays attached to the paper that measured it.
