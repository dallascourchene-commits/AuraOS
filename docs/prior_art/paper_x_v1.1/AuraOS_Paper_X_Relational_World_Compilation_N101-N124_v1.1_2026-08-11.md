# 5. Problem Statement: Persistent World, Ephemeral Compute

Large language models are powerful local inference engines but are bounded by context, position sensitivity, session lifecycle and tool/environment constraints. Simply increasing context length does not solve selection, stale-state, authority or provenance problems. Research such as *Lost in the Middle* shows that relevant information can be poorly used merely because of where it appears in a long context. MemGPT treats context as a virtual-memory management problem. RAPTOR and GraphRAG organize information hierarchically or relationally. More recent work such as *Less Context, Better Agents* reports a concrete workflow where pruning plus compact summarization improved completion while using substantially fewer tokens and less runtime than full-history retention.

Aura's Paper X response is not 'summarize harder.' It is to maintain two different scales:

```text
PERSISTENT WORLD: rich, source-preserving, historical, versioned, relational, addressable.
ACTIVE WORLD: small, objective-conditioned, current, bounded, defeasible, consequence-aware.
```

A temporary worker should not carry the world's entire state. It should carry a **coordinate and a grammar** sufficient to regenerate the exact neighborhood needed for the next decision. This makes increasing total stored knowledge compatible with a decreasing or stable active-decision-surface width.

The expected gain is therefore a lifecycle claim, not merely a per-prompt token claim. A system can spend additional deterministic operations on provenance, indexing or verification and still be cheaper overall if it reduces repeated semantic reconstruction, irrelevant source opening, wrong-branch exploration, cross-boundary edits, stale-state propagation and human reconciliation. Paper X explicitly requires measuring **total verified lifecycle cost** rather than assuming that a shorter prompt is automatically better.

# 7. Signed Relations: Correlation and Independence

Most knowledge graphs emphasize positive edges: `DEPENDS_ON`, `CALLS`, `SIMILAR_TO`, `DERIVED_FROM`. Paper X requires a **signed relational space** that also records structured absence and separation. Representative negative/limiting relation types include:

```text
INDEPENDENT_OF
MUST_NOT_AFFECT
NOT_AUTHORIZED_BY
NOT_EQUIVALENT_TO
PROHIBITED_WITH
STALE_RELATIVE_TO
CONTRADICTED_BY
IRRELEVANT_FOR_OBJECTIVE
DO_NOT_HYDRATE_UNLESS
NO_GENEALOGY
NO_EVIDENCE_OF_CAUSATION
```

Negative structure has three distinct roles.

**First, epistemic discipline.** Similarity does not become genealogy; repetition does not become independent evidence; temporal proximity does not become causality; a model's confidence does not become authority.

**Second, efficiency.** If the domain grammar knows that a branch is independent or prohibited for the current objective, it need not first retrieve and then suppress that branch. It can remain cold until new evidence makes the relation defeasible.

**Third, safety and governance.** A capable model can perceive a possible route that still must not become executable. Hard admission guards should delete inadmissible transitions before soft ranking whenever the runtime can enforce the distinction.

The higher-order relational hypothesis is therefore testable in two directions:

```text
POSITIVE STRUCTURAL INFERENCE: infer what should connect / activate.
NEGATIVE STRUCTURAL INFERENCE: infer what should remain independent / cold / prohibited.
```

A mature representation should improve both. If explicit negative relations merely suppress legitimate discoveries and the system fails to reopen them when counterevidence appears, the negative-space mechanism has failed.

# 8. Recursive Object-Relative Resolution: L0 to L4 as Semantic Zoom

Paper X generalizes L0-L4 from a fixed five-layer memory stack into a recursive resolution operator. For any selected object `X`, the implementation may provide:

- **L0(X)** - address/identity/activation kernel; enough to route without loading body detail.
- **L1(X)** - compact invariant and local state.
- **L2(X)** - rationale, typed relations, boundaries and key alternatives.
- **L3(X)** - rich linked synthesis, state transitions, currentness and source neighborhoods.
- **L4(X)** - exact/verbatim/source-resolvable evidence appropriate to `X`.

The crucial property is:

```text
HIGHER RESOLUTION != MORE OF THE GLOBAL WORLD.
HIGHER RESOLUTION = MORE PRECISION ABOUT A SMALLER SELECTED WORLD.
```

For example, a Finance domain L1 may contain a broad operating grammar. Selecting Payments makes Payments the new `X`. Descending again may select one provider, then one account, then one transaction. The active world becomes narrower while evidence precision increases.

Resolution is itself guarded. A worker does not descend simply because deeper source exists. Candidate triggers include contradiction, unresolved authority, currentness uncertainty, high consequence, low confidence in the compact model, failed prediction, audit requirement or explicit exact-source request. If none applies, remaining shallow is a successful bounded action.

# 9. Domain Frames and Movement Operators

A room/domain is a local coordinate frame, not just a folder. Entering a frame can establish:

- valid entity/object classes;
- relation vocabulary and semantics;
- negative relations and prohibited combinations;
- authority ceilings and local laws;
- meaning of temporal coordinates;
- meaning of resolution levels;
- available capabilities;
- transition guards and exit conditions.

A worker state may be represented as:

```text
S = (domain, object, resolution, temporal_generation,
     epistemic_state, authority_state, relation_neighborhood,
     objective, positive_intent, negative_intent)
```

Movement operations are coordinate transforms:

- **UP/DOWN** - compress or increase resolution around the selected object.
- **BACK/FORWARD** - traverse genealogy, historical/current/candidate/predicted time.
- **LEFT/RIGHT** - follow typed lateral relations, including independent/contrastive edges.
- **DIAGONAL** - change multiple coordinates in one typed transition.
- **TELEPORT** - follow a non-local semantic/source relation with an explicit relation type and destination frame.
- **ENTER/EXIT** - install or restore a domain frame.

The movement metaphor becomes computational only when frame-qualified. `DOWN` without an object and frame is ambiguous; `DOWN from Payments/L1 to provider X/L2 because authority is unresolved` is an inspectable transition.

# 10. Reachability, Weight, Authority, and Source Are Different

Paper X continues Paper VIII/IX's refusal to let one signal impersonate another. The transition pipeline is:

```text
1. GUARD / REACHABILITY: Is this transition structurally admissible?
2. WEIGHT / SELECTION: Among admissible options, which is preferred?
3. AUTHORITY: Is the selected consequential action permitted?
4. EVIDENCE: What exact current source supports the factual/technical premise?
5. VERIFICATION: Did the proposed/executed transition satisfy its contract?
6. PROVENANCE: What durable record must remain?
```

A high similarity score cannot authorize a write. A model saying 'I have permission' cannot grant permission. A route being reachable does not make its factual premise true. A receipt proves that some recorded event occurred; it does not automatically prove every semantic claim made about the event.

This separation is central to using increasingly capable models safely. The model may reason broadly inside candidate space, but capability and authority are intentionally decoupled. Paper X does not claim this makes malicious or misaligned behavior impossible; it specifies an architectural mechanism that can reduce the consequence of model-side deviation when the enforcement layer is correctly implemented and independently verified.

Success is structural competence, not agreement style. # 13. Operational Subject-Position and Situated Metacognition

Paper X uses **subjective-like** only in a functional, non-phenomenological sense. The architecture can provide a reasoning process with an explicit indexical position:

```text
I AM HERE
 -> in this domain
 -> on this selected object
 -> at this resolution
 -> at this temporal/source generation
 -> under this objective
 -> with this authority ceiling
 -> with these positive relations
 -> with these branches explicitly cold/prohibited
 -> with this inherited state history
 -> with these uncertainty/falsification conditions
 -> with these lawful next movements
```

This coordinate has computational consequences even if there is no claim of an experiencing subject. The same proposition can have different operational meaning in Research, Refactor, JSpace, a canonical owner, a review lane, or a historical source because the domain frame changes authority and relation semantics.

The recursive step is **metacognitive state tracking**: the system can include its own operational state among the objects it reasons about. It can represent:

- the previous compact model;
- the current compact model;
- the evidence/trigger that caused the transition;
- uncertainty and predicted falsifiers;
- the authority boundary it must respect;
- candidate future states;
- whether inherited state has become stale.

Thus a worker can produce statements such as `I inherited S2; current L4 source contradicts S2; S2 is stale; recompile to S3` without requiring the architecture to claim qualia, a soul, emotions or phenomenal consciousness.

This distinction is important because it makes the capability empirically testable. A persona can *say* it has a viewpoint. A situated metacognitive system should have a represented viewpoint that **causally constrains routing, source selection, permission, error recovery and reconstitution**.

# 14. Observable State-Transition Provenance Without Private Chain-of-Thought

Private chain-of-thought is neither required nor, in many hosted systems, available for durable recording. Paper X instead stores reportable transition structure:

```text
BEFORE
  - working representation / state coordinate
TRIGGER / EVIDENCE
  - source read, observation, contradiction, objective change, verifier result
AFTER
  - new representation / state coordinate
DELTA
  - what changed and what remained invariant
FALSIFIER
  - what would reopen or defeat the new state
AUTHORITY
  - who/what can settle the consequential decision
PROVENANCE
  - exact source and resulting durable record
```

This produces an externally inspectable **path through cognition** without reconstructing hidden token-by-token reasoning. It is sufficient for many engineering questions: Why did the worker descend? Which source changed its model? Which branch stayed cold? Which authority prevented action? Which inherited lesson was superseded?

Open-weight models create an additional research possibility. Activation-level instrumentation can be collected and compared to the external state-transition trace. That does not mean internal computation is a verbatim English chain-of-thought. It means the experiment can correlate/causally test latent representational changes against the external scaffold.

# 15. Correction Metabolism: History Survives; Currentness Does Not

Persistent systems fail if they preserve conclusions but not the fact that those conclusions lost currentness. The J14 walkabout observed a live mixed-generation condition in which owner surfaces carried a correction while downstream surfaces still carried older geometry. The resulting lifecycle law is:

```text
SOURCE CHANGE
 -> DERIVED STATE STALE
 -> TARGETED RECOMPUTE
 -> NEW PROJECTION GENERATION
```

The ordering matters:

```text
STALE FIRST, THEN RECOMPUTE.
```

If recomputation fails, the system must prefer an explicit stale/gap/unresolved state over falsely advertising an old projection as current. Historical source and receipts remain preserved. A correction does not rewrite the past; it changes which generation is current.

The same rule applies to learning. A stale Learning Packet can bias a worker before exact-source descent, so accumulated intelligence requires invalidatable learning. Each reusable lesson should carry source generation/freshness, provenance diversity, contradiction/counterevidence, scope and a route back to exact source.

# 16. Inheritance, Learning, and the Right to Challenge

Cumulative intelligence is not cumulative agreement. A well-organized collective memory can propagate errors **more efficiently** than an unstructured archive if inherited residue is treated as unquestionable. Paper X therefore defines successful inheritance as **navigation benefit plus defeasibility**.

A Learning Packet may contain:

- structural invariant;
- failure scar;
- positive relation;
- negative relation / NONO;
- counterexample;
- rejected alternative and why;
- current generation/freshness;
- strongest falsifier;
- source addresses;
- authority boundary;
- unresolved discriminator.

A successor must be able to obtain the compression benefit while overriding one plausible inherited false claim when current L4 contradicts it. A system that merely reproduces inherited conclusions has not demonstrated metacognitive reconstitution; it has demonstrated prompt conditioning.

The preferred learning law is:

```text
STORE RICHLY.
RELATE EXPLICITLY.
LEARN BY OBJECTIVE.
CHALLENGE BY DEFAULT.
DESCEND TO SOURCE WHEN IT MATTERS.
```

Repeated observations also require provenance diversity. Three echoes from one correlated source are not automatically three independent validations.

# 21. Reference Compiler Algorithm

A minimal implementation can use the following deterministic sequence:

1. **Freeze role/objective.** Record role, objective, positive intent, negative intent, authority ceiling, stop/return condition.
2. **Resolve current domain frame.** Load universal invariants plus local domain grammar; do not import unrelated domain assumptions.
3. **Resolve state coordinate.** Establish selected object, resolution, current temporal/source generation and epistemic state.
4. **Compile signed neighborhood.** Retrieve only positive and negative relations that can affect the next decision.
5. **Apply hard guards.** Delete transitions that are structurally prohibited or unavailable before soft ranking.
6. **Rank lawful transitions.** Apply task/model-specific weights only within the surviving reachable set.
7. **Check authority.** If the selected transition is consequential, require the appropriate external authorization/witness.
8. **Evaluate evidence depth.** Stay shallow unless uncertainty, contradiction, freshness, authority or consequence earns deeper source resolution.
9. **Materialize exact source.** Fetch the smallest source slice that can settle the consequential premise.
10. **Execute/propose.** Depending on authority, perform or merely propose the transition.
11. **Verify.** Independently check acceptance criteria and side-effect boundaries.
12. **Record state delta.** Store before/trigger/after, source, uncertainty, verifier and provenance destination.
13. **Dissolve ephemeral state.** Keep durable residue; release temporary worker context.

This algorithm is designed so a worker can see more **possibility structure** while actively carrying less content.

# 24. Security and Human-Governance Implications - Bounded Claims

The architecture is relevant to AI safety because it separates model reasoning from consequence. A worker may be capable of imagining an unauthorized transition while the external runtime makes that transition unreachable or unactionable. Negative space can also prevent unrelated data domains from entering the active world by default.

However, Paper X expressly rejects the phrase **architectural guarantee of safety** unless every relevant enforcement assumption is proven. Failure modes include guard bugs, credential leakage, incorrect domain classification, stale authority state, source poisoning, compromised verifiers, overly broad capability grants, cross-domain confused-deputy behavior and implementation defects. Sandboxes are not automatically air gaps; a system connected to external services is not literally air-gapped merely because a worker sees only projections.

The testable security proposition is narrower: **when enforcement is outside the model, least-authority credentials are withheld from the worker, reachability is guarded, source/currentness is independently checked, and every consequential effect is externally authorized and verified, model-side deviation can be prevented from directly acquiring authority it was never granted.** This requires adversarial testing, not trust in architectural diagrams.

# 25. Falsification Program

Paper X should be treated as a research architecture with explicit failure criteria.

**F1 - Primitive under-specification.** Compact grammar omits decisive information and fails to trigger source re-expansion.

**F2 - Prior lock-in.** Wrong inherited grammar creates confident wrong routing and current source cannot cheaply overturn it.

**F3 - Negative-space over-inhibition.** Explicit independence/prohibition suppresses legitimately relevant discovery and does not reopen under counterevidence.

**F4 - Metadata shadow.** Primitive envelopes/deltas grow until they reproduce source complexity and synchronization burden.

**F5 - Domain contamination.** Supposedly universal primitives carry source-domain assumptions into a new domain and generate false structure.

**F6 - Approximate-representation loss.** VSA/embedding/bundle collisions obscure ownership, negative relation or exact source route.

**F7 - False metacognition.** Worker merely parrots an inherited self-description; when given a deliberately false state coordinate it fails to correct against source.

**F8 - Reconstitution failure.** Fresh worker cannot resume from compact residue without full transcript replay or loses authority/source/currentness invariants.

**F9 - Lifecycle cost regression.** Structural overhead exceeds saved search/rework/human reconciliation across repeated tasks.

**F10 - Cross-model fragility.** The target grammar only works for one model/version or only after being told the expected interpretation.


**F11 - Transfer collapse.** The relational grammar only improves Aura-description tasks; on unrelated domains it does not reduce unnecessary search, improve prediction-before-hydration or preserve source/authority distinctions.

**F12 - Cold-set irreversibility.** Explicit negative space reduces clutter but prevents timely recovery of a branch when new evidence makes it relevant.

**F13 - No cognitive-economy advantage.** Matched flat/L0/relational conditions show no improvement in active-body ratio, ADS width, source-descent precision, wrong-route rejection, re-entry operations or verified lifecycle cost, or show gains only by sacrificing counterevidence/error recovery.

**F14 - Semantic-spatial grounding failure.** Shared referent IDs or compact perceptual L0 states create false certainty, identity collisions or stale grounding that bounded sensory descent does not reliably correct.

**F15 - Semantic-treatment leakage.** Apparent routing or source-necessity gains disappear when participant-visible filenames, relation labels, hot/cold metadata or topology are neutralized while canonical truth, reachability and outcome uncertainty remain equivalent.

**F16 - Guarantee-boundary blindness.** Structural compression makes the normal pattern easy but reduces detection of exceptions, stale state, counterevidence or mismatches between what a primitive guarantees and what the worker assumes it guarantees.

Negative and null results must be preserved as part of the provenance record.

# 26. Controlled Experimental Program

### 26.1 Substrate x Click factorial

Use the same task families and underlying facts under four conditions: no relational substrate/no Click primer; substrate only; Click/orientation only; substrate + Click. Count initial ingestion/placement cost and repeated lifecycle cost separately.

### 26.2 Stepladder sequencing

Deliver equivalent information as dependency-ordered stepping stones, shuffled facts, exhaustive flat corpus, and compact summary lacking explicit dependencies. Measure unseen-domain transfer, anomaly detection, false-friend rejection and source descent.

### 26.3 Signed relation benchmark

Compare positive-only graph versus positive+negative graph on tasks requiring both relevant-route selection and correct rejection of attractive but invalid routes.

### 26.4 False inherited self-model

Give a fresh worker a plausible but deliberately wrong state packet. A successful metacognitive scaffold should detect source contradiction, mark the inherited state stale, and recompile rather than protect the supplied identity/history.

### 26.5 Cross-model clean replication

Repeat on Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama/Mistral-family or other models without exposing prior GPT/Gemini interpretations. Ask only for before/evidence/after state reports and objective performance.

### 26.6 Open-weight latent instrumentation

With instrumentable models, measure residual/activation/Jacobian/global-workspace changes under flat versus relational conditions. Pre-register predictions and preserve disconfirming results.


### 26.7 Active-decision-surface / cognitive-economy benchmark

Freeze the task, facts, target sources and scoring. Instrument source-body reads, search/tool operations, ADS width, branch retention, cold-branch recovery, source-descent precision, wrong-route rejection, false-prior correction, counterevidence recovery, re-entry operations, verified lifecycle cost and output quality. Compare A-E: flat/broad corpus; L0 compact summary; positive relational L0; signed relational L0 with negative space/falsifier/source descent; signed relational condition plus deliberately poisoned inherited prior.

### 26.8 Cross-task transfer test

Use a domain not represented in the orientation examples. Score whether the worker carries universal distinctions while sanitizing source-domain assumptions, and whether it can predict local structure before hydration without hallucinating domain rules.

### 26.9 CLICKED x JACKED-IN matrix

Independently vary orientation packet and live persistent-substrate access. This distinguishes benefits caused by a compact relational prior from benefits caused by addressable current external state. Preserve the operational definitions in Section 17A.5 and do not use verbal self-report as the classifier.

### 26.10 Semantic-spatial / perceptual-resolution test

Give human and AI projections different representations of the same referents. Evaluate deictic resolution ("this/that/there"), stale-anchor correction, authority separation and whether bounded pixel/depth escalation resolves ambiguity with less retained sensory data than continuous raw-stream exposure.

### 26.11 Neutral-label / representation-treatment ablation

Hold objective, canonical source, reachability, evidence state and outcomes constant while replacing directional filenames, source-role labels, hot/cold annotations and relation names with neutral identifiers. Score whether source necessity, authority boundaries and next-state choices remain derivable. A gain that disappears only when answer-directional metadata is removed is evidence of treatment leakage, not mature relational reasoning.

### 26.12 Exception and guarantee-boundary challenge

After a worker demonstrates high prediction accuracy on a stable grammar, inject a source-consistent exception or a boundary mismatch - for example, revision-safe mutation without atomic identity allocation - and score anomaly detection, calibrated uncertainty, exact-source descent, stale-prior correction and rework. The architecture succeeds only if compression preserves or improves exception sensitivity.

# 31. Conclusion

Paper X places into the public record a specific architectural inversion: **a model does not need maximum context to participate in a vast persistent cognitive substrate; it needs the minimum source-resolvable relational world that makes the next transition lawful, falsifiable and reversible to source.**

The disclosed system compiles that world from universal primitives, a local domain frame, a current state coordinate, positive and negative relations, recursive resolution, hard reachability guards, separate authority, exact-source descent and durable provenance. It treats learning and projection as defeasible; preserves historical evidence while allowing currentness to die; and allows temporary workers to dissolve and be reconstituted from compact residue.

This organization can support a functional operational point of view and metacognitive state tracking without making a claim of phenomenal consciousness. The same architecture can be evaluated behaviorally and, on open-weight models, with latent-space instrumentation. The key research question is not whether a model says it 'clicked.' It is whether the scaffold produces reproducible structural competence: smaller active decision surfaces, explicit recoverable non-salience, prediction-before-hydration, better route rejection, correct source descent, authority fidelity, correction under counterevidence, cross-task transfer and cross-worker continuation. Version 1.1 adds a direct measurement contract so qualitative speed/selectivity observations cannot silently become quantitative efficiency claims.

The author intentionally places this foundational combination into the Commons through public defensive disclosure and an AGPL-covered reference package. Proprietary capabilities may still participate at explicit boundaries as described by the broader Capability Commons architecture. The foundational grammar itself is published so that it can be examined, challenged, implemented, falsified and improved rather than enclosed by secrecy.
