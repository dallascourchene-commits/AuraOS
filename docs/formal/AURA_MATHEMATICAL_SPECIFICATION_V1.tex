\begin{abstract}
This document formalizes a bounded subset of AuraOS: (i) four Prime Directives as engineering boundary constraints, (ii) the six-slot finite-state routing morphology \texttt{DIR $\rightarrow$ ASP $\rightarrow$ CLASS $\rightarrow$ SUBJ $\rightarrow$ VOICE $\rightarrow$ STEM}, and (iii) the 3--6--9 fleet pattern as a phase-staggering scheduling grammar. The specification is deliberately narrower than a claim of a universal physical or mathematical law. Routing is not truth, capability is not authority, a receipt is not truth, and numerical phase labels do not establish numerological or causal performance claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Source Boundary}
The normative inputs are the staged Founder Bio \& Vision record and the active experimental-canonical Six-Slot FST Routing \& Lazy-Hydration Contract. Their controlling design rule is to compile the smallest source-resolvable relational world sufficient for the objective. A compact route is therefore an addressable projection, never an independent truth plane. Exact source evidence may defeat summaries or cached projections.

Let a governed transition state be
\[
S=(R,I,C,A,P,T),
\]
where $R$ is relational/source context, $I$ identity and provenance, $C$ capability, $A$ authority, $P$ the proposed operation, and $T$ the successor/receipt context. A consequential transition is admissible only when all required boundary predicates are true.

\section{Four Prime Directives as Boundary Constraints}
The source record gives the recursive governance sequence
\[
\mathrm{RELATION}\rightarrow\mathrm{RECOGNITION}\rightarrow\mathrm{GOVERNED\ CARE}\rightarrow\mathrm{RECIPROCITY/RECEIPT}\rightarrow\mathrm{RELATION}',
\]
corresponding respectively to Gakina-awiiya, Giwaabamin, Gi-zaagi'in, and Miigwech. This is treated as a constitutional engineering kernel, not as a mystical or universal physical law.

\subsection{D1: Gakina-awiiya --- Relation}
No operational object is context-free:
\[
D_1(S)=1 \iff \operatorname{src}(S)\neq\varnothing\land \operatorname{id}(S)\neq\varnothing\land \operatorname{constraints}(S)\text{ are represented}.
\]
If $D_1=0$, the system may route to discovery/source recovery, but must not promote the state as source-closed.

\subsection{D2: Giwaabamin --- Recognition}
Recognition preserves identity, provenance, ownership, currentness, and boundaries:
\[
D_2(S)=1 \iff \operatorname{identityBound}(S)\land\operatorname{provenanceBound}(S)\land\operatorname{current}(S).
\]
Addressability, retrieval, ranking, hashes, or similarity do not satisfy $D_2$ by themselves.

\subsection{D3: Gi-zaagi'in --- Governed Care}
Capability does not create permission. For a consequential proposal $P$,
\[
D_3(S,P)=1 \iff \operatorname{capable}(S,P)\land\operatorname{authorized}(S,P)\land\operatorname{negativeSpaceOK}(S,P)\land\operatorname{leaseOK}(S,P).
\]
Thus $\operatorname{capable}(S,P)\not\Rightarrow\operatorname{authorized}(S,P)$. Failure maps to refusal or non-mutating staging.

\subsection{D4: Miigwech --- Reciprocity and Receipt}
A transition is not closed merely because output bytes exist:
\[
D_4(S,S')=1 \iff \operatorname{attribution}(S,S')\land\operatorname{deltaBound}(S,S')\land\operatorname{evidenceBound}(S,S')\land\operatorname{authorityBound}(S,S').
\]
The receipt records a transition; it does not become the truth source.

\subsection{Prime-directive admissibility}
For consequential work,
\[
\mathcal{A}(S,P)=D_1(S)\land D_2(S)\land D_3(S,P)\land D_4^{\ast}(S,P),
\]
where $D_4^{\ast}$ requires that the transition can close with a source-bound successor receipt. If $\mathcal{A}=0$, mutation is denied or held for human disposition.

\subsection{Lazy hydration invariant}
Let $L_0,\ldots,L_4$ denote increasing evidence resolution, with $L_4$ canonical source evidence. For objective $O$ and sufficiency predicate $\kappa$,

\section{3--6--9 Phase Concurrency}
\subsection{Interpretation boundary}
The source material defines 3--6--9 as an orchestration grammar, not numerology and not a universal physical harmonic. The following results formalize only properties that follow from triad partitioning plus lease/currentness constraints.

Let a triad be $\tau_i=\{w_{i,1},w_{i,2},w_{i,3}\}$. Then
\[
\mathcal{W}_3=\tau_1,\qquad \mathcal{W}_6=\tau_1\cup\tau_2,\qquad \mathcal{W}_9=\tau_1\cup\tau_2\cup\tau_3.
\]

\subsection{Lease model}
A mutation lease $\lambda(w,r,g)$ binds worker $w$ to resource $r$ at source generation $g$. Exclusive mutation requires
\[
\lambda(w_i,r,g)\land\lambda(w_j,r,g)\land i\neq j\Rightarrow\Reject.
\]
Read-only workers may share a resource without acquiring mutation authority.

\subsection{Theorem 1: intra-phase collision exclusion}
\textbf{Claim.} If every mutation requires an exclusive atomic lease on $(r,g)$, two workers in the same 3-, 6-, or 9-worker phase cannot both lawfully commit to the same $(r,g)$.

\textbf{Proof.} Assume distinct $w_i,w_j$ both lawfully commit. Lawful commit requires both leases. Lease exclusivity maps the conjunction to $\Reject$, contradicting lawful commit. Hence at most one mutation is admissible. $\square$

\subsection{Theorem 2: stale-generation exclusion}
For current generation $g'=\operatorname{head}(r)$, a worker based on $g$ may commit only if
\[
g=g'\quad\text{or}\quad\operatorname{rebaseVerified}(g\rightarrow g')=1.
\]
Increasing worker cardinality therefore increases candidate parallelism, not authority over stale state.

\subsection{Theorem 3: triad composability}
Let triad $i$ emit bounded result $R_i=(\Delta_i,E_i,A_i,H_i)$ containing delta, evidence, authority state, and receipt hash. If mutation supports are pairwise disjoint,
\[
\operatorname{supp}(\Delta_i)\cap\operatorname{supp}(\Delta_j)=\varnothing\Rightarrow\Delta_i\circ\Delta_j=\Delta_j\circ\Delta_i.
\]
Thus three bounded triads may execute concurrently and reconcile at nine-worker closure without a global write lock. Overlapping supports require lease/currentness adjudication instead of the commutativity assumption.

\subsection{What these proofs do not establish}
They do not prove that the labels 3, 6, and 9 create a physical speedup, an optimal fleet size, universal zero-collision behavior, or a universal performance law. Empirical claims require a bound workload, agent count, lease schedule, collision definition, and run receipt.

\section{Conformance Conditions}
An implementation conforms only if it: (1) preserves six-slot order; (2) uses bounded candidates rather than guessing unresolved STEM identity; (3) separates routing from truth/source authority; (4) can descend to exact source evidence; (5) denies consequential VOICE transitions without authority and lease; (6) binds mutation to current source generation or verified rebase; (7) treats 3--6--9 as scheduling grammar rather than numerology; (8) produces source-bound receipts without treating receipts as truth; and (9) leaves human sovereign disposition outside model consensus or route score.
