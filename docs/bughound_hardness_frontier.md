## Eight-axis evaluator-only vector

`(interprocedural_hops, cross_file_span, trace_depth, statefulness, control_flow_ambiguity, historical_signal_scarcity, oracle_cost, patch_distance)`

The first three retain raw measurable counts before policy binning. The remaining five are explicit ordinal evaluator classifications in `{0,1,2}`. The policy is versioned and content-digested; changing thresholds changes benchmark identity.

Default count bins are deliberately benchmark-local, not universal truth:
- interprocedural hops: `<2`, `2-4`, `>=5`;
- cross-file span: `1`, `2-3`, `>=4`;
- trace depth: `1-2`, `3-5`, `>=6`.

## Noncompensatory frontier

Every run reports:
- overall recall interval;
- each named stratum separately;
- every axis-high slice separately;
- hard-tail slice (at least two HIGH axes);
- extreme frontier (at least four HIGH axes).

A hardness-coverage claim is admitted only when the hard-tail and **every** axis-high slice have enough cases and their Wilson lower confidence bounds clear the declared floors. High performance on easy cases cannot pay an unpaid hard slice.

`AggregateRecall != HardTailRecall`.
`EasyVolume != HardCaseEvidence`.
`OneHardAxisPass != OtherHardAxisPass`.
`HardnessLabel != SolverEvidence`.
`PolicyThreshold != UniversalDifficultyTruth`.
`HistoricalBlindHardnessCoverage != ZeroDayDiscovery`.
`HardnessCoverage != GeneralizedRealWorldSuperiority`.
