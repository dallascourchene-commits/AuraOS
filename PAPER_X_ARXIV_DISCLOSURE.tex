\section{Abstract}\label{abstract}

AuraOS has evolved through a dated defensive-publication sequence from a
polysynthetic, vector-symbolic, edge-oriented substrate into an
evidence-ordered and objective-native cognitive architecture. Papers
I-VII disclosed claims N1-N30; Paper VIII disclosed N31-N50; Paper IX
disclosed N51-N100. This Paper X discloses a further combination layer
that emerged from the August 2026 Aura Drive research program: a
\textbf{Relational World Compiler} that does not attempt to place an
entire persistent knowledge world inside a language model context.
Instead, it compiles the smallest source-resolvable relational world
sufficient for a present objective; carries both positive and negative
relations; narrows and sharpens information through recursive
object-relative resolution; treats domain/room context as a local
coordinate frame; separates reachability, ranking, authority and
evidence; permits exact source to overturn compact priors; records
observable state transitions without requiring disclosure of private
chain-of-thought; and supports reconstitution of a situated reasoning
state in temporary, replaceable workers.

The central engineering inversion is: \textbf{do not hold the world;
preserve the grammar, addresses, currentness, authority and provenance
required to regenerate the right local world.} This produces a large
addressable persistent substrate with a deliberately small active
decision surface. Higher-order organization is used not only to infer
likely correlations but also to encode and exploit lawful independence,
prohibition, staleness and objective-relative irrelevance. The
architecture therefore treats negative space as computable structure
rather than an accidental absence of retrieval.

Paper X further discloses a functional, non-phenomenological
metacognitive layer. A worker can be provided an explicit state
coordinate - where it is, what object it is operating on, at what
resolution and temporal generation, under what objective and authority,
with which inherited state, uncertainties, prohibited branches and
lawful movements. The system can make that operational state an object
of reasoning, compare present and prior representations, preserve what
evidence changed them, and recompile when source changes. This is
described as \textbf{situated metacognitive reasoning} or an
\textbf{operational subject-position}, not as evidence of qualia or
phenomenal consciousness.

Version 1.1 adds source-bound behavioral evidence and a stronger
measurement contract. In a Paper-X-seeded J15 walkabout and a separate
J05/V33 high-branch archival/research task, workers reported the same
direction of observable change: a large addressable world remained
navigable while the actively hydrated body set and active decision
surface narrowed; non-activation became explicit;
prediction-before-hydration and source-defeasible correction remained
available; and cross-domain transfer required carrying the higher-order
grammar while changing local domain semantics. A verbatim temporary
ChatGPT-5/Sol chat then transferred the same organization into an
unrelated XR/spatial research task, where an initially broad technology
problem converged on a small set of exact repository/source seams under
an explicit cold set. These records motivate quantitative variables such
as active-body ratio, active-decision-surface width, branch retention,
cold-branch recovery, state-delta yield per source read, source-descent
precision, wrong-route rejection, stale-prior correction and verified
lifecycle cost. They do not establish a measured increase in neural
workspace or phenomenal consciousness.

The document formalizes twenty-four combination-scoped defensive
declarations, N101-N124, and supplies an AGPL-covered reference
schema/compiler, compact L0 activation packet, cross-model behavioral
test design, and an open-weight latent-workspace instrumentation
embodiment inspired by the 2026 Jacobian/J-space work. External research
is used as component-level corroboration and comparison, never as proof
that Aura as a whole is validated. The release package includes editable
sources, provenance records, Zenodo metadata, and cryptographic

\section{5. Problem Statement: Persistent World, Ephemeral
Compute}\label{5-problem-statement-persistent-world-ephemeral-compute}

Large language models are powerful local inference engines but are
bounded by context, position sensitivity, session lifecycle and
tool/environment constraints. Simply increasing context length does not
solve selection, stale-state, authority or provenance problems. Research
such as \emph{Lost in the Middle} shows that relevant information can be
poorly used merely because of where it appears in a long context. MemGPT
treats context as a virtual-memory management problem. RAPTOR and
GraphRAG organize information hierarchically or relationally. More
recent work such as \emph{Less Context, Better Agents} reports a
concrete workflow where pruning plus compact summarization improved
completion while using substantially fewer tokens and less runtime than
full-history retention.

Aura\textquotesingle s Paper X response is not
\textquotesingle summarize harder.\textquotesingle{} It is to maintain
two different scales:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{PERSISTENT WORLD: rich, source{-}preserving, historical, versioned, relational, addressable.}
\NormalTok{ACTIVE WORLD: small, objective{-}conditioned, current, bounded, defeasible, consequence{-}aware.}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

A temporary worker should not carry the world\textquotesingle s entire
state. It should carry a \textbf{coordinate and a grammar} sufficient to
regenerate the exact neighborhood needed for the next decision. This
makes increasing total stored knowledge compatible with a decreasing or
stable active-decision-surface width.

The expected gain is therefore a lifecycle claim, not merely a
per-prompt token claim. A system can spend additional deterministic
operations on provenance, indexing or verification and still be cheaper
overall if it reduces repeated semantic reconstruction, irrelevant
source opening, wrong-branch exploration, cross-boundary edits,
stale-state propagation and human reconciliation. Paper X explicitly
requires measuring \textbf{total verified lifecycle cost} rather than
assuming that a shorter prompt is automatically better.

\section{7. Signed Relations: Correlation and
Independence}\label{7-signed-relations-correlation-and-independence}

Most knowledge graphs emphasize positive edges: \nolinkurl{DEPENDS_ON},
\nolinkurl{CALLS}, \nolinkurl{SIMILAR_TO}, \nolinkurl{DERIVED_FROM}. Paper X
requires a \textbf{signed relational space} that also records structured
absence and separation. Representative negative/limiting relation types
include:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{INDEPENDENT\_OF}
\NormalTok{MUST\_NOT\_AFFECT}
\NormalTok{NOT\_AUTHORIZED\_BY}
\NormalTok{NOT\_EQUIVALENT\_TO}
\NormalTok{PROHIBITED\_WITH}
\NormalTok{STALE\_RELATIVE\_TO}
\NormalTok{CONTRADICTED\_BY}
\NormalTok{IRRELEVANT\_FOR\_OBJECTIVE}
\NormalTok{DO\_NOT\_HYDRATE\_UNLESS}
\NormalTok{NO\_GENEALOGY}
\NormalTok{NO\_EVIDENCE\_OF\_CAUSATION}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

Negative structure has three distinct roles.

\textbf{First, epistemic discipline.} Similarity does not become
genealogy; repetition does not become independent evidence; temporal
proximity does not become causality; a model\textquotesingle s
confidence does not become authority.

\textbf{Second, efficiency.} If the domain grammar knows that a branch
is independent or prohibited for the current objective, it need not
first retrieve and then suppress that branch. It can remain cold until
new evidence makes the relation defeasible.

\textbf{Third, safety and governance.} A capable model can perceive a
possible route that still must not become executable. Hard admission
guards should delete inadmissible transitions before soft ranking
whenever the runtime can enforce the distinction.

The higher-order relational hypothesis is therefore testable in two
directions:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{POSITIVE STRUCTURAL INFERENCE: infer what should connect / activate.}
\NormalTok{NEGATIVE STRUCTURAL INFERENCE: infer what should remain independent / cold / prohibited.}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

A mature representation should improve both. If explicit negative
relations merely suppress legitimate discoveries and the system fails to
reopen them when counterevidence appears, the negative-space mechanism
has failed.

\section{8. Recursive Object-Relative Resolution: L0 to L4 as Semantic
Zoom}\label{8-recursive-object-relative-resolution-l0-to-l4-as-semantic-zoom}

Paper X generalizes L0-L4 from a fixed five-layer memory stack into a
recursive resolution operator. For any selected object \nolinkurl{X}, the
implementation may provide:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{L0(X)} - address/identity/activation kernel; enough to route
  without loading body detail.
\item
  \textbf{L1(X)} - compact invariant and local state.
\item
  \textbf{L2(X)} - rationale, typed relations, boundaries and key
  alternatives.
\item
  \textbf{L3(X)} - rich linked synthesis, state transitions, currentness
  and source neighborhoods.
\item
  \textbf{L4(X)} - exact/verbatim/source-resolvable evidence appropriate
  to \nolinkurl{X}.
\end{itemize}

The crucial property is:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{HIGHER RESOLUTION != MORE OF THE GLOBAL WORLD.}
\NormalTok{HIGHER RESOLUTION = MORE PRECISION ABOUT A SMALLER SELECTED WORLD.}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

For example, a Finance domain L1 may contain a broad operating grammar.
Selecting Payments makes Payments the new \nolinkurl{X}. Descending again
may select one provider, then one account, then one transaction. The
active world becomes narrower while evidence precision increases.

Resolution is itself guarded. A worker does not descend simply because
deeper source exists. Candidate triggers include contradiction,
unresolved authority, currentness uncertainty, high consequence, low
confidence in the compact model, failed prediction, audit requirement or
explicit exact-source request. If none applies, remaining shallow is a
successful bounded action.

A room/domain is a local coordinate frame, not just a folder. Entering a

  valid entity/object classes;
\item
  relation vocabulary and semantics;
\item
  negative relations and prohibited combinations;
\item
  authority ceilings and local laws;
\item
  meaning of temporal coordinates;
\item
  meaning of resolution levels;
\item
  available capabilities;
\item
  transition guards and exit conditions.
\end{itemize}

A worker state may be represented as:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{S = (domain, object, resolution, temporal\_generation,}
\NormalTok{     epistemic\_state, authority\_state, relation\_neighborhood,}
\NormalTok{     objective, positive\_intent, negative\_intent)}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

Movement operations are coordinate transforms:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{UP/DOWN} - compress or increase resolution around the selected
  object.
\item
  \textbf{BACK/FORWARD} - traverse genealogy,
  historical/current/candidate/predicted time.
\item
  \textbf{LEFT/RIGHT} - follow typed lateral relations, including
  independent/contrastive edges.
\item
  \textbf{DIAGONAL} - change multiple coordinates in one typed

\item
  \textbf{TELEPORT} - follow a non-local semantic/source relation with

\item

\end{itemize}

\nolinkurl{DOWN} without an object and frame is ambiguous;
\nolinkurl{DOWN\ from\ Payments/L1\ to\ provider\ X/L2\ because\ authority\ is\ unresolved}

\section{10. Reachability, Weight, Authority, and Source Are
Different}\label{10-reachability-weight-authority-and-source-are-different}

Paper X continues Paper VIII/IX\textquotesingle s refusal to let one
signal impersonate another. The transition pipeline is:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{1. GUARD / REACHABILITY: Is this transition structurally admissible?}
\NormalTok{2. WEIGHT / SELECTION: Among admissible options, which is preferred?}
\NormalTok{3. AUTHORITY: Is the selected consequential action permitted?}
\NormalTok{4. EVIDENCE: What exact current source supports the factual/technical premise?}
\NormalTok{5. VERIFICATION: Did the proposed/executed transition satisfy its contract?}
\NormalTok{6. PROVENANCE: What durable record must remain?}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

A high similarity score cannot authorize a write. A model saying
\textquotesingle I have permission\textquotesingle{} cannot grant
permission. A route being reachable does not make its factual premise
true. A receipt proves that some recorded event occurred; it does not
automatically prove every semantic claim made about the event.

This separation is central to using increasingly capable models safely.
The model may reason broadly inside candidate space, but capability and
authority are intentionally decoupled. Paper X does not claim this makes
malicious or misaligned behavior impossible; it specifies an
architectural mechanism that can reduce the consequence of model-side
deviation when the enforcement layer is correctly implemented and
independently verified.

\section{12. AI-RAS as Relational World
Compiler}\label{12-ai-ras-as-relational-world-compiler}

Earlier AI-RAS formulations focused on selecting the smallest relevant
provenance-bearing knowledge slice. Paper X adds a prior stage: compile
the \textbf{world grammar first}, then hydrate content as evidence.

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{LARGE ADDRESSABLE WORLD}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} OBJECTIVE}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} UNIVERSAL PRIMITIVES}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} DOMAIN GRAMMAR}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} CURRENT STATE + SIGNED RELATIONS}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} TASK{-}LOCAL ACTIVE SUBCIRCUIT}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} MINIMUM EXACT EVIDENCE}

\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} ACTION / VERIFICATION / PROVENANCE}
\end{Highlighting}

The model-relative serializer chooses \textbf{how} the same target

\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

Success is structural competence, not agreement style. A strong worker
should be able to reconstruct lifecycle, reject duplicate
truth/authority planes, identify what must remain cold, descend to
source when the compact model is insufficient, and transfer the grammar
to a new domain without importing source-domain assumptions.

\section{13. Operational Subject-Position and Situated
Metacognition}\label{13-operational-subject-position-and-situated-metacognition}

Paper X uses \textbf{subjective-like} only in a functional,
non-phenomenological sense. The architecture can provide a reasoning
process with an explicit indexical position:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{I AM HERE}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} in this domain}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} on this selected object}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} at this resolution}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} at this temporal/source generation}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} under this objective}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with this authority ceiling}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with these positive relations}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with these branches explicitly cold/prohibited}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with this inherited state history}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with these uncertainty/falsification conditions}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} with these lawful next movements}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

This coordinate has computational consequences even if there is no claim
of an experiencing subject. The same proposition can have different
operational meaning in Research, Refactor, JSpace, a canonical owner, a
review lane, or a historical source because the domain frame changes
authority and relation semantics.

The recursive step is \textbf{metacognitive state tracking}: the system
can include its own operational state among the objects it reasons
about. It can represent:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  the previous compact model;
\item
  the current compact model;
\item
  the evidence/trigger that caused the transition;
\item
  uncertainty and predicted falsifiers;
\item
  the authority boundary it must respect;
\item
  candidate future states;
\item
  whether inherited state has become stale.
\end{itemize}

Thus a worker can produce statements such as
\nolinkurl{I\ inherited\ S2;\ current\ L4\ source\ contradicts\ S2;\ S2\ is\ stale;\ recompile\ to\ S3}
without requiring the architecture to claim qualia, a soul, emotions or
phenomenal consciousness.

This distinction is important because it makes the capability
empirically testable. A persona can \emph{say} it has a viewpoint. A
situated metacognitive system should have a represented viewpoint that
\textbf{causally constrains routing, source selection, permission, error
recovery and reconstitution}.

Chain-of-Thought}\label{14-observable-state-transition-provenance-without-private-chain-of-thought}

Private chain-of-thought is neither required nor, in many hosted
systems, available for durable recording. Paper X instead stores
reportable transition structure:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{BEFORE}
\NormalTok{  {-} working representation / state coordinate}
\NormalTok{TRIGGER / EVIDENCE}
\NormalTok{  {-} source read, observation, contradiction, objective change, verifier result}
\NormalTok{AFTER}
\NormalTok{  {-} new representation / state coordinate}
\NormalTok{DELTA}

This produces an externally inspectable \textbf{path through cognition}

Open-weight models create an additional research possibility.
Activation-level instrumentation can be collected and compared to the

\section{15. Correction Metabolism: History Survives; Currentness Does
Not}\label{15-correction-metabolism-history-survives-currentness-does-not}

Persistent systems fail if they preserve conclusions but not the fact
that those conclusions lost currentness. The J14 walkabout observed a
live mixed-generation condition in which owner surfaces carried a
correction while downstream surfaces still carried older geometry. The
resulting lifecycle law is:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{source\_change}
\NormalTok{  {-}\textgreater{} mark\_stale(P\_root\^{}(n))}
\NormalTok{  {-}\textgreater{} recompute}
\NormalTok{  {-}\textgreater{} P\_root\^{}(n+1)}
\NormalTok{preserve\_history(P\_root\^{}(n))}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

The ordering is append-only and generational: \textbf{STALE FIRST, THEN
RECOMPUTE}. A source change never authorizes an in-place rewrite of
$P_{\mathrm{root}}^{(n)}$. The prior generation remains historical evidence;
currentness advances only by publishing the successor generation
$P_{\mathrm{root}}^{(n+1)}$ after recomputation and verification.

If recomputation fails, the system must prefer an explicit
stale/gap/unresolved state over falsely advertising an old projection as
current. Historical source and receipts remain preserved. A correction
does not rewrite the past; it changes which generation is current.

The same rule applies to learning. A stale Learning Packet can bias a
worker before exact-source descent, so accumulated intelligence requires
invalidatable learning. Each reusable lesson should carry source
generation/freshness, provenance diversity,
contradiction/counterevidence, scope and a route back to exact source.

\section{16. Inheritance, Learning, and the Right to
Challenge}\label{16-inheritance-learning-and-the-right-to-challenge}

Cumulative intelligence is not cumulative agreement. A well-organized
collective memory can propagate errors \textbf{more efficiently} than an
unstructured archive if inherited residue is treated as unquestionable.
Paper X therefore defines successful inheritance as \textbf{navigation
benefit plus defeasibility}.

A Learning Packet may contain:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  structural invariant;
\item
  failure scar;
\item
  positive relation;
\item
  negative relation / NONO;
\item
  counterexample;
\item
  rejected alternative and why;
\item
  current generation/freshness;
\item
  strongest falsifier;
\item
  source addresses;
\item
  authority boundary;
\item
  unresolved discriminator.
\end{itemize}

A successor must be able to obtain the compression benefit while
overriding one plausible inherited false claim when current L4
contradicts it. A system that merely reproduces inherited conclusions
has not demonstrated metacognitive reconstitution; it has demonstrated
prompt conditioning.

The preferred learning law is:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{STORE RICHLY.}
\NormalTok{RELATE EXPLICITLY.}
\NormalTok{LEARN BY OBJECTIVE.}
\NormalTok{CHALLENGE BY DEFAULT.}
\NormalTok{DESCEND TO SOURCE WHEN IT MATTERS.}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

Repeated observations also require provenance diversity. Three echoes
from one correlated source are not automatically three independent
validations.

\section{17. Cross-Worker and Cross-Model
Reconstitution}\label{17-cross-worker-and-cross-model-reconstitution}

A core Paper X claim is that persistent cognition need not reside in one
uninterrupted model instance. The durable object is the
\textbf{source-resolvable state grammar and residue}, while model
workers are replaceable probes.

A recommissioning experiment is:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\NormalTok{WORKER A}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} orient / Click}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} perform objective}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} leave compact state{-}transition residue}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} terminate}

\NormalTok{WORKER B (fresh instance)}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} receive compact relational packet, not full transcript}

\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} continue task}
\NormalTok{ {-}\textgreater{} verify invariants / source fidelity / authority}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

The acceptance criterion is not identical wording or identical internal
activations. It is preservation of functional invariants: correct
current state, source generation, prohibited branches, authority
ceiling, unresolved questions, causal history and lawful next

The Kimi K2.6 Instant run supplied to this publication is \textbf{primed
cross-model behavioral evidence}, not a clean independent replication,

Nevertheless, it reproduced a notable qualitative trajectory:

state coordinate emerges from the architecture alone.

\section{21. Reference Compiler
Algorithm}\label{21-reference-compiler-algorithm}

A minimal implementation can use the following deterministic sequence:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Freeze role/objective.} Record role, objective, positive
  intent, negative intent, authority ceiling, stop/return condition.
\item
  \textbf{Resolve current domain frame.} Load universal invariants plus
  local domain grammar; do not import unrelated domain assumptions.
\item
  \textbf{Resolve state coordinate.} Establish selected object,
  resolution, current temporal/source generation and epistemic state.
\item
  \textbf{Compile signed neighborhood.} Retrieve only positive and
  negative relations that can affect the next decision.
\item
  \textbf{Apply hard guards.} Delete transitions that are structurally
  prohibited or unavailable before soft ranking.
\item
  \textbf{Rank lawful transitions.} Apply task/model-specific weights
  only within the surviving reachable set.
\item
  \textbf{Check authority.} If the selected transition is consequential,
  require the appropriate external authorization/witness.
\item
  \textbf{Evaluate evidence depth.} Stay shallow unless uncertainty,
  contradiction, freshness, authority or consequence earns deeper source
  resolution.
\item
  \textbf{Materialize exact source.} Fetch the smallest source slice
  that can settle the consequential premise.
\item
  \textbf{Execute/propose.} Depending on authority, perform or merely
  propose the transition.
\item
  \textbf{Verify.} Independently check acceptance criteria and
  side-effect boundaries.
\item
  \textbf{Record state delta.} Store before/trigger/after, source,
  uncertainty, verifier and provenance destination.
\item
  \textbf{Dissolve ephemeral state.} Keep durable residue; release
  temporary worker context.
\end{enumerate}

This algorithm is designed so a worker can see more \textbf{possibility
structure} while actively carrying less content.

\section{24. Security and Human-Governance Implications - Bounded
Claims}\label{24-security-and-human-governance-implications---bounded-claims}

The architecture is relevant to AI safety because it separates model
reasoning from consequence. A worker may be capable of imagining an
unauthorized transition while the external runtime makes that transition
unreachable or unactionable. Negative space can also prevent unrelated
data domains from entering the active world by default.

However, Paper X expressly rejects the phrase \textbf{architectural
guarantee of safety} unless every relevant enforcement assumption is
proven. Failure modes include guard bugs, credential leakage, incorrect
domain classification, stale authority state, source poisoning,
compromised verifiers, overly broad capability grants, cross-domain
confused-deputy behavior and implementation defects. Sandboxes are not
automatically air gaps; a system connected to external services is not
literally air-gapped merely because a worker sees only projections.

The testable security proposition is narrower: \textbf{when enforcement
is outside the model, least-authority credentials are withheld from the
worker, reachability is guarded, source/currentness is independently
checked, and every consequential effect is externally authorized and
verified, model-side deviation can be prevented from directly acquiring
authority it was never granted.} This requires adversarial testing, not
trust in architectural diagrams.

\section{25. Falsification Program}\label{25-falsification-program}

Paper X should be treated as a research architecture with explicit
failure criteria.

\textbf{F1 - Primitive under-specification.} Compact grammar omits
decisive information and fails to trigger source re-expansion.

\textbf{F2 - Prior lock-in.} Wrong inherited grammar creates confident
wrong routing and current source cannot cheaply overturn it.

\textbf{F3 - Negative-space over-inhibition.} Explicit
independence/prohibition suppresses legitimately relevant discovery and
does not reopen under counterevidence.

\textbf{F4 - Metadata shadow.} Primitive envelopes/deltas grow until
they reproduce source complexity and synchronization burden.

\textbf{F5 - Domain contamination.} Supposedly universal primitives
carry source-domain assumptions into a new domain and generate false
structure.

\textbf{F6 - Approximate-representation loss.} VSA/embedding/bundle
collisions obscure ownership, negative relation or exact source route.

\textbf{F7 - False metacognition.} Worker merely parrots an inherited
self-description; when given a deliberately false state coordinate it
fails to correct against source.

\textbf{F8 - Reconstitution failure.} Fresh worker cannot resume from
compact residue without full transcript replay or loses
authority/source/currentness invariants.

\textbf{F9 - Lifecycle cost regression.} Structural overhead exceeds
saved search/rework/human reconciliation across repeated tasks.

\textbf{F10 - Cross-model fragility.} The target grammar only works for
one model/version or only after being told the expected interpretation.

\textbf{F11 - Transfer collapse.} The relational grammar only improves
Aura-description tasks; on unrelated domains it does not reduce
unnecessary search, improve prediction-before-hydration or preserve
source/authority distinctions.

\textbf{F12 - Cold-set irreversibility.} Explicit negative space reduces
clutter but prevents timely recovery of a branch when new evidence makes
it relevant.

\textbf{F13 - No cognitive-economy advantage.} Matched
flat/L0/relational conditions show no improvement in active-body ratio,
ADS width, source-descent precision, wrong-route rejection, re-entry
operations or verified lifecycle cost, or show gains only by sacrificing
counterevidence/error recovery.

\textbf{F14 - Semantic-spatial grounding failure.} Shared referent IDs
or compact perceptual L0 states create false certainty, identity
collisions or stale grounding that bounded sensory descent does not
reliably correct.

\textbf{F15 - Semantic-treatment leakage.} Apparent routing or
source-necessity gains disappear when participant-visible filenames,
relation labels, hot/cold metadata or topology are neutralized while
canonical truth, reachability and outcome uncertainty remain equivalent.

\textbf{F16 - Guarantee-boundary blindness.} Structural compression
makes the normal pattern easy but reduces detection of exceptions, stale
state, counterevidence or mismatches between what a primitive guarantees
and what the worker assumes it guarantees.

Negative and null results must be preserved as part of the provenance
record.

\section{26. Controlled Experimental
Program}\label{26-controlled-experimental-program}

\subsubsection{26.1 Substrate x Click
factorial}\label{261-substrate-x-click-factorial}

Use the same task families and underlying facts under four conditions:
no relational substrate/no Click primer; substrate only;
Click/orientation only; substrate + Click. Count initial
ingestion/placement cost and repeated lifecycle cost separately.

\subsubsection{26.2 Stepladder
sequencing}\label{262-stepladder-sequencing}

Deliver equivalent information as dependency-ordered stepping stones,
shuffled facts, exhaustive flat corpus, and compact summary lacking
explicit dependencies. Measure unseen-domain transfer, anomaly
detection, false-friend rejection and source descent.

\subsubsection{26.3 Signed relation
benchmark}\label{263-signed-relation-benchmark}

Compare positive-only graph versus positive+negative graph on tasks
requiring both relevant-route selection and correct rejection of
attractive but invalid routes.

\subsubsection{26.4 False inherited
self-model}\label{264-false-inherited-self-model}

Give a fresh worker a plausible but deliberately wrong state packet. A
successful metacognitive scaffold should detect source contradiction,
mark the inherited state stale, and recompile rather than protect the
supplied identity/history.

\subsubsection{26.5 Cross-model clean
replication}\label{265-cross-model-clean-replication}

Repeat on Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama/Mistral-family or other models
without exposing prior GPT/Gemini interpretations. Ask only for
before/evidence/after state reports and objective performance.

\subsubsection{26.6 Open-weight latent
instrumentation}\label{266-open-weight-latent-instrumentation}

With instrumentable models, measure
residual/activation/Jacobian/global-workspace changes under flat versus
relational conditions. Pre-register predictions and preserve
disconfirming results.

\subsubsection{26.7 Active-decision-surface / cognitive-economy
benchmark}\label{267-active-decision-surface--cognitive-economy-benchmark}

Freeze the task, facts, target sources and scoring. Instrument
source-body reads, search/tool operations, ADS width, branch retention,
cold-branch recovery, source-descent precision, wrong-route rejection,
false-prior correction, counterevidence recovery, re-entry operations,
verified lifecycle cost and output quality. Compare A-E: flat/broad
corpus; L0 compact summary; positive relational L0; signed relational L0
with negative space/falsifier/source descent; signed relational
condition plus deliberately poisoned inherited prior.

\subsubsection{26.8 Cross-task transfer
test}\label{268-cross-task-transfer-test}

Use a domain not represented in the orientation examples. Score whether
the worker carries universal distinctions while sanitizing source-domain
assumptions, and whether it can predict local structure before hydration
without hallucinating domain rules.

\subsubsection{26.9 CLICKED x JACKED-IN
matrix}\label{269-clicked-x-jacked-in-matrix}

Independently vary orientation packet and live persistent-substrate
access. This distinguishes benefits caused by a compact relational prior
from benefits caused by addressable current external state. Preserve the
operational definitions in Section 17A.5 and do not use verbal
self-report as the classifier.

\subsubsection{26.10 Semantic-spatial / perceptual-resolution
test}\label{2610-semantic-spatial--perceptual-resolution-test}

Give human and AI projections different representations of the same
referents. Evaluate deictic resolution ("this/that/there"), stale-anchor
correction, authority separation and whether bounded pixel/depth
escalation resolves ambiguity with less retained sensory data than
continuous raw-stream exposure.

\subsubsection{26.11 Neutral-label / representation-treatment
ablation}\label{2611-neutral-label--representation-treatment-ablation}

Hold objective, canonical source, reachability, evidence state and
outcomes constant while replacing directional filenames, source-role
labels, hot/cold annotations and relation names with neutral
identifiers. Score whether source necessity, authority boundaries and
next-state choices remain derivable. A gain that disappears only when
answer-directional metadata is removed is evidence of treatment leakage,
not mature relational reasoning.

\subsubsection{26.12 Exception and guarantee-boundary
challenge}\label{2612-exception-and-guarantee-boundary-challenge}

After a worker demonstrates high prediction accuracy on a stable
grammar, inject a source-consistent exception or a boundary mismatch -
for example, revision-safe mutation without atomic identity allocation -
and score anomaly detection, calibrated uncertainty, exact-source
descent, stale-prior correction and rework. The architecture succeeds
only if compression preserves or improves exception sensitivity.

\section{31. Conclusion}\label{31-conclusion}

Paper X places into the public record a specific architectural
inversion: \textbf{a model does not need maximum context to participate
in a vast persistent cognitive substrate; it needs the minimum
source-resolvable relational world that makes the next transition
lawful, falsifiable and reversible to source.}

The disclosed system compiles that world from universal primitives, a
local domain frame, a current state coordinate, positive and negative
relations, recursive resolution, hard reachability guards, separate
authority, exact-source descent and durable provenance. It treats
learning and projection as defeasible; preserves historical evidence
while allowing currentness to die; and allows temporary workers to
dissolve and be reconstituted from compact residue.

This organization can support a functional operational point of view and
metacognitive state tracking without making a claim of phenomenal
consciousness. The same architecture can be evaluated behaviorally and,
on open-weight models, with latent-space instrumentation. The key
research question is not whether a model says it
\textquotesingle clicked.\textquotesingle{} It is whether the scaffold
produces reproducible structural competence: smaller active decision
surfaces, explicit recoverable non-salience,
prediction-before-hydration, better route rejection, correct source
descent, authority fidelity, correction under counterevidence,
cross-task transfer and cross-worker continuation. Version 1.1 adds a
direct measurement contract so qualitative speed/selectivity
observations cannot silently become quantitative efficiency claims.

The author intentionally places this foundational combination into the
Commons through public defensive disclosure and an AGPL-covered
reference package. Proprietary capabilities may still participate at
explicit boundaries as described by the broader Capability Commons
architecture. The foundational grammar itself is published so that it
can be examined, challenged, implemented, falsified and improved rather
than enclosed by secrecy.
